Rooftop Networking, the Institute’s ambient coordination framework, is entering public beta across the central district. The system requires no cabling, no account, and no meaningful reason to call it a network.

## Infrastructure that arrives itself

Participating nodes establish presence on available elevated surfaces, exchange short-range vocal packets, and depart without warning. The architecture is naturally redundant: when one pigeon leaves, there is generally another pigeon.

Initial coverage is strongest near train stations, public squares, and buildings whose maintenance teams have stopped trying.

We will publish findings as the network expands, consolidates, or collectively decides to move two roofs east.
