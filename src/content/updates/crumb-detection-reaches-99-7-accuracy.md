The Institute’s Adaptive Crumb Detection program has achieved 99.7% accuracy in a controlled evaluation spanning concrete, brick, granite, and the small metal tables placed outside coffee shops.

## A smaller target. A larger ambition.

The benchmark included sourdough, laminated pastry, seeded roll, and several substances that were ultimately determined not to be bread. Pigeon One detected viable targets in shadow, partial occlusion, and moderate foot traffic.

Researchers attribute the remaining 0.3% to conditions described in the report as “somebody had already eaten it.”

The detection stack will now enter extended validation against bagels, which present unique density and geometry challenges.
