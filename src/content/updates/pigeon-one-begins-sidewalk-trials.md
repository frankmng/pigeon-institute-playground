Today, The Pigeon Institute began sidewalk trials of Pigeon One, our most complete urban mobility platform to date. The program will evaluate navigation, pedestrian avoidance, curb transition, and opportunistic carbohydrate acquisition under normal city conditions.

## Built for the unpredictable

Sidewalks are among the most demanding environments in modern aviation. Foot traffic changes without notice. Outdoor furniture migrates. A promising croissant can disappear in seconds.

Pigeon One responds through continuous observation and a willingness to walk slightly faster when necessary. In early testing, the system maintained composure through bicycles, umbrellas, rolling luggage, and one small dog with unrealistic ambitions.

> Flight is only half the journey. The other half is standing near a bench.

The trial will continue through the autumn. No road closures are expected, requested, or remotely justified.
